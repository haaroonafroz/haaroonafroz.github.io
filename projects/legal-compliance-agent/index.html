REGULATORY AI · AGENTIC RAG · COMPLIANCE AUTOMATION

How can AI turn regulatory change into traceable policy updates?

I built an inspectable compliance workflow that monitors new regulatory material, retrieves the internal policies it may affect, identifies gaps, drafts policy amendments and audits those drafts before producing a final report. Rather than relying on one opaque agent, the system decomposes the process into explicit stages with stored state, retrieval evidence and observable model calls.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Regulatory change creates an information matching problem before it becomes a drafting problem.

New regulations arrive as long, heterogeneous documents mixed with irrelevant website content. Understanding their impact requires connecting specific obligations to the right internal policies before anyone can decide what should change.

01

Regulatory noise

Crawled sources contain navigation, duplicated content and material that may have nothing to do with a company's actual compliance scope.

02

Policy mapping

A new obligation only becomes actionable once it is connected to the internal policies and departments that it may affect.

03

Generated changes need scrutiny

A plausible policy amendment is not enough. Drafts need a separate review stage capable of identifying unsupported reasoning, inconsistencies and mismatches.

The goal was not to create a single autonomous legal chatbot. I designed the workflow so that discovery, retrieval, analysis, drafting and audit remain distinct and inspectable stages. This makes intermediate decisions easier to trace, evaluate and improve independently.

04 · SPECIALIZED WORKFLOW

Each stage answers a different question.

Specialization keeps high-volume filtering, information retrieval, legal comparison, drafting and quality control from collapsing into one oversized prompt.

HORIZON SCANNER

What changed?

Retrieves unprocessed regulatory chunks, groups them by document and reconstructs the original text only when chunk sequence and document identity are consistent.

LIBRARIAN

Which internal policies matter?

Searches the internal policy collection using dense and sparse retrieval, metadata filters and rank fusion to surface the policy sections most related to the new regulation.

RELEVANCE CHECK

Is this worth processing?

Acts as an early gate that filters irrelevant or noisy regulatory material before invoking the more expensive reasoning stages.

ANALYST

Where is the compliance gap?

Compares the regulation with retrieved internal policy context and produces an explicit gap analysis.

REDLINER

What should change?

Converts the identified gaps into proposed policy amendments rather than generating an unstructured legal summary.

AUDITOR

Does the draft survive review?

Reviews the analysis and proposed amendments for unsupported claims, weak reasoning and inconsistencies before the workflow finalizes its report.

05 · RETRIEVAL

Legal matching needs more than semantic similarity.

Regulations and internal policies can express similar obligations with different terminology, while exact legal terms can also carry important meaning. The retrieval layer therefore combines dense semantic retrieval with sparse lexical retrieval.

01

Dense retrieval

Embeddings capture semantic similarity between regulatory passages and internal policy sections even when their wording differs.

02

Sparse retrieval

Lexical signals preserve the value of specific terminology, names and exact expressions that semantic vectors alone may underweight.

03

Rank fusion + reranking

Qdrant combines candidate sets using reciprocal rank fusion. When configured, a reranking stage further narrows the retrieved policy context before it reaches the Analyst.

06 · QUALITY CONTROL

Generation is not the final step.

07 · MODEL ROUTING

Not every workflow step needs the same model budget.

The system separates low-cost filtering from reasoning-heavy legal analysis so model capacity can be allocated according to the job being done.

01

Relevance filtering

Fast, lower-cost model.

The relevance gate is deliberately routable to a cheaper model because it performs a narrow classification task rather than deep policy analysis.

02

Analysis and drafting

Stronger reasoning models.

Analyst, Redliner and Auditor stages are routed to the primary workflow model because they require substantially deeper reasoning over regulation and policy context.

03

Provider flexibility

OpenAI or Gemini per workflow configuration.

The workflow supports configurable providers, including per-step Gemini thinking levels, allowing reasoning depth to vary according to each stage.

08 · OBSERVABILITY

If a compliance workflow cannot be inspected, it is difficult to improve.

The architecture favors explicit workflow stages and trace-friendly integrations so retrieval, model calls and agent decisions can be examined independently rather than hidden inside one autonomous loop.

01

Langfuse

Captures model traces and execution context for inspection across workflow runs.

02

Arize Phoenix

Provides OpenTelemetry-based tracing for the LlamaIndex and retrieval workflow.

03

Intermediate state

Gap analyses, proposed updates, retrieval context and audit feedback remain separate workflow artifacts instead of disappearing inside an opaque chain.

09 · ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Designing for traceability, cost and retrieval quality.

01

One large agent or explicit workflow?

Explicit workflow.

Specialized stages make failures easier to locate and allow retrieval, analysis, drafting and auditing to evolve separately.

02

Dense retrieval or hybrid retrieval?

Hybrid.

Legal text benefits from both semantic matching and lexical precision, so the retrieval layer combines dense and sparse candidate sets.

03

Process every scraped page?

Filter first.

A dedicated relevance gate prevents expensive analysis stages from spending tokens on irrelevant regulatory material.

04

Accept the first generated draft?

Audit and revise.

The drafting model and review model have separate responsibilities, with bounded revision when the Auditor rejects a proposal.
